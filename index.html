Day 2

Classics + Nørrebro offbeat + CopenHill

Mon 21 Sep
Morning

GRØD porridge at Torvehallerne (79–99 DKK), then Rosenborg Castle (140 DKK) + King's Garden, Strøget, Round Tower (50–60 DKK), Christiansborg tower (free — best panorama in the city).

Midday

EatDinner at Reffen street-food market (harbour bus 91/992 from Nyhavn, ~15 min; mains 80–130 DKK). Or POW Vegan Pizzeria / Souls if the weather turns.
NightMonday: Mikkeller & Friends (Nørrebro, 40 taps) or Mikkeller Bar Viktoriagade (Vesterbro, to 23:00, beers ~65 DKK). Cheap-and-cheerful: Escobar (Blågårdsgade, daily till 2am).

Day 5

Morskie Oko — the big Tatra day

Thu 24 Sep
Early

"Morskie Oko" minibuses from Zakopane bus station (ul. Jagiellońska), from ~06:00, every ~20 min, 15 PLN cash. Park entry 11 PLN.

The hike

Paved 8–9 km climb to Morskie Oko (2–2.5h each way), passing the Wodogrzmoty Mickiewicza waterfalls at km 3 — 30–40 minutes in, viewpoints from the road and a stone bridge. Optional add-on: Czarny Staw (+2 km each way, wilder). From 2026 horse carts only cover the first 2.5 km to the falls.

Gem · alternative hikeIf you'd rather avoid Morskie Oko's crowds, Rusinowa Polana — the sunrise meadow with the full High-Tatras panorama — starts from the same Palenica Białczańska trailhead (blue trail, ~50 min steep) or gentler from Wierch Poroniec (~1h). Park rules ban pre-dawn trail walking (Mar–Nov), so go at first light for morning mist instead. Both hikes share the same bus.
Evening

Recovery at Termy Chochołowskie — the 19:00 evening slot is 69 PLN for 3 hours of thermal pools (~15 km, taxi or Chochołów bus).

EatKawiarnia Filiżanki (4.8★, veg-friendly, 20–40 PLN) or Szczypiorek (vegetarian delivery — lazy group night in). Budget: Bar Górski milk-bar with labelled veg options.
Also in the areaSiklawica waterfall via Dolina Strążyska (walkable from Krupówki, 1.5–2h round trip, ~9–11 PLN entry — best after rain; dry late-Sept can reduce it to a trickle) and Wielka Krokiew ski jump (free viewpoint, 20–30 min walk). If you want a full alternative valley day: Dolina Kościeliska — buses every 15–20 min to Kiry, flat canyon walk, Mroźna Cave, Smreczyński pond, 4–5h.
Day 6

Slovakia day — Belianska Cave, Ždiar, cable car — then Krakow

Fri 25 Sep
Move07:30 direct cross-border bus from Zakopane station toward Poprad (new line since Jan 2026, 4× daily; also the A15 at 06:32) — stops at Ždiar and Tatranská Kotlina en route to Starý Smokovec. ~€5. Passport in pocket.
Morning

Off at Tatranská Kotlina: 25-min educational-trail climb to Belianska Cave — the only show cave in the High Tatras, 70-min guided tour, 874 stairs, €15 (photo fees abolished 2026). Entries at 9:30 / 11:00 / 12:30 — target the first two. (Closed Mondays — Friday is fine.)

Midday

Bus/train along the Tatra electric railway to Starý Smokovec, the vintage spa town. Lunch, then the alpine hit:

  • Budget — Hrebienok funicular (€13 return) + waterfall walk.
  • Best value — Tatranská Lomnica → Skalnaté Pleso cable car (€34 return): terraces, chamois, half the crowds of the Polish side.
  • Splurge — Lomnický štít (2,634 m): €89, 50 minutes on the summit, pre-book in the Gopass app.
Return

15:17 A15 back from Starý Smokovec → Zakopane 16:35 (backup: minibus to Łysa Polana + border walk + SAD bus, last ~18:15). Collect bags, evening bus to Krakow (2h–2h30, 25–45 PLN; FlixBus till ~23:45). Check in.

GemŽdiar — the Goral folk village with black-wooded architecture — is a stop on the same A15 line (09:45 inbound / 15:45 outbound), so a 20-minute wander is nearly free if timings line up.
EatLate Krakow arrival: Vegab (vegan kebabs, Starowiślna) or Glonojad (canteen veg, till 22:00, 20–35 PLN).
Day 7

Krakow — classics + Zakrzówek + mound sunset + Kazimierz

Sat 26 Sep
Morning

Wawel (grounds + cathedral free; State Rooms 95 PLN if booked) → Rynek Główny, Cloth Hall, St Mary's (15 PLN), Floriańska Gate.

Midday — the local's Krakow

Tram 8/11/18/22 to Zakrzówek — a turquoise flooded limestone quarry with 30-m cliffs and a 2.3 km cliff-path loop (~40 min), free, with a view of Wawel across the water. (The lifeguarded swimming zone closes 7 Sep, but the walk and viewpoints are year-round.) Then tram out or walk back via the riverside.

Afternoon options — pick one
  • Nowa Huta (tram 4/10/16/22, ~20 min): the Stalinist showpiece suburb — Plac Centralny, Aleja Róż (wider than the Champs-Élysées), the Lord's Ark church built in defiance of the regime. DIY by tram or a ~90–150 PLN foundation walking tour.
  • Kazimierz deep-dive instead: Szeroka, Remu'h synagogue, vintage shops, and pre-dinner drinks.
Sunset

Kościuszko Mound (bus 100/101) — the best panorama over the whole city, 34 PLN with fort exhibitions; open to 19:00.

Evening

Kazimierz: cross the Father Bernatek footbridge to Podgórze — ten gravity-defying bronze acrobats suspended over the deck, best at blue hour — then back for the night out (below).

EatKrowarzywa vegan burgers (16–32 PLN), Veganic (Polish veg classics, weekend breakfast buffet 40 PLN), zapiekanka at Plac Nowy (12–18 PLN, ask for veg).
Night · Saturday is the big oneStart at Plac Nowy (draught 12–16 PLN, legal outdoor drinking, zapiekanka window till 2–3am) → Alchemia (candle-lit institution, cellar gigs, arrive before 20:00 for a table) → Singer (sewing-machine tables) or Hevre (1896 prayer-house cocktail bar) → Prozak 2.0 club (3 dancefloors, ~10 PLN entry — check the week's events) or Propaganda till 4am. Between bars: Pijalnia Wódki i Piwa — communist-themed shot bars, vodka 5–7 PLN.
Swap logicIf Auschwitz is booked for Saturday instead of Sunday, invert this and the next day. Rain swap: Wieliczka Salt Mine (131–156 PLN, 17°C underground, book bilety.kopalnia.pl). Tyniec Abbey by riverside bike is the other half-day gem if the group prefers monastery-calm to communist-blocks.

Day 11

Bohinj + Mostnica Gorge + Slap Mostnica — the waterfall day

Wed 30 Sep
Morning

Bus to Ribčev Laz at Lake Bohinj (~2–2.5h via Bled, €8–12). The wilder sister of Bled — the lake loop and St John's Church are free.

Midday — hidden gem hike

Flat 20–25-min walk to Stara Fužina, then Mostnica Gorge — a slot canyon the colour of green glass, "the Devil's Bridge" booth at the entry (€3 in October, cash). Gorge loop ~1.5h; push on through golden October beech forest to Slap Mostnica (21 m, via the Koča na Vojah hut) for a 3–3.5h / 12 km / 300 m total outing. Slippery after rain — real shoes.

Afternoon

The panoramic boat across the lake still runs (part of the €41 Bohinj Standard Package with the church), or loop the lake and claim a jetty.

EatPicnic from Ljubljana, or a gostilna plate of štruklji (rolled dumplings — many vegetarian variants). Back in Ljubljana: Veganika (open Wed evenings).
Weather swapThis is the flexible outdoor day — trade with Postojna/Škocjan (all-weather, 10°C) if Wednesday pours.
Day 12

Cave day — the choice: Postojna vs Škocjan

Thu 1 Oct
Choice A · Postojna + PredjamaThe classic: 3.7 km underground train ride + walking galleries (~€30, postojnska-jama.eu; bus ~1h, €5 each way). Combo with cliff-perched Predjama Castle €42–45 — though the castle is 9 km from the station with few buses, so it works best as bus-to-cave + decide on the day (taxi share or skip).
Choice B · Škocjan Caves — the traveller pickUNESCO since 1986, the Murmuring Cave canyon up to 146 m deep crossed on a vertigo bridge — ~100,000 visitors a year vs Postojna's ~700,000, groups capped small. ~€23 for a 1.5h guided tour (park-skocjanske-jame.si). Train Ljubljana→Divača ~1h (€5–8) + a marked 35–40-min walk from the station. Travellers who've done both mostly pick Škocjan if choosing one; doing Postojna Sunday-plus-Škocjan later in the week is overkill — pick your flavour.
Evening

Last full Ljubljana night — riverside drinks (beer €3–5), and Gujžina (vegetarian Slovenian cuisine, Mestni trg 19 — verify it has reopened) if you want the one proper Slovenian dinner.

NightKino Šiška — check the gig list; the region's best indie venue, café with 90+ craft beers till 23:00. Otherwise riverside bars; Thursday is the city's student night, the liveliest of your Ljubljana stretch.

Day 15

Plitvice — with the Rastoke waterfall-village stop

Sun 4 Oct
Early

06:45 FlixBus toward Plitvice (€14.50, 2h–2h45) — and here's the gem play: pick a departure that stops at Slunj (Brioni, AP Varaždin and Croatia Bus services do; check on Traveling.com/GetByBus and tell the driver). Rastoke — a village of watermills strung across a waterfall junction — is a 7–10-min walk from the Slunj stop; the public paths are free, the protected mill estate ~€5–6, 1–1.5h is plenty. Then hop the next bus ~30 min up the road to Plitvice Entrance 1.

In the park

€23 October ticket (timed, buy the day before at the official webshop; includes the Kozjak boat + panoramic train). Circuit: Veliki Slap → Lower Lakes canyon → boat → Upper Lakes → train. 5–6h. Slick boardwalks — decent shoes.

Evening

Return bus to Zagreb (know the time before you set off). Sunday dinner: Vegehop (daily 12–20) or BioMania (daily 12–23) — most of the rest closes Sundays.

Timing noteIf the Rastoke stop means taking a slightly later arrival at Plitvice, it's worth it — Rastoke at 09:00 with nobody there is exactly the traveller-vibe you asked for; Plitvice after 10:00 is tour-bus hour regardless, so the order works.

Before you fly

Apps to download tonight

All verified current in 2025–26. Install while you still have home wifi; several need a card or an account. Set up Google Pay/Apple Pay on your phone — most of Europe's transit now taps it.

The everywhere backbone

FlixBus (Krakow→Ljubljana, Ljubljana→Zagreb, Zagreb→Plitvice — offline tickets, no account needed to buy). Google Maps offline areas for all six countries (navigation only — no offline transit routing, keep data). Organic Maps as the fully-free offline backup. Omio only for comparing; it adds service fees — book with operators.

Denmark — changed in 2025!

Rejsebillet — the new national ticket app (replaces the retiring DOT Tickets; needs phone number + payment card). Rejsekort app for tap-in/tap-out if you'll ride a lot. Rejseplanen — the journey planner, free, excellent. Copenhagen Card City Guide — the card is now 100% digital through this app.

Poland

jakdojade — city transit in Krakow incl. trams and airport train tickets (card/BLIK/tap; also sells Koleje Małopolskie rail since 2025 — but NOT Zakopane buses). e-podroznik — the intercity-bus marketplace for Zakopane legs (no account to search, BLIK/card to buy). Bolt — Krakow taxis (rides 20–50 PLN, covers the airport). PKP Intercity — update it before paying (new payment processor since Aug 2026).

Slovakia

CP (cp.sk) — the national planner, now sells ZSSK train tickets in-app. IDeme vlakom — ZSSK's app, covers the Tatra Electric Railway (single ~€4, 24h ~€6). Gopass.travel — Tatra cable cars; buy 3+ days ahead to save up to €10; online tickets are QR, not the plastic card.

Slovenia

Urbana card (not an app — buy the €2 card at kiosks/Urbanomat machines; €1.50 rides with 90-min transfers; buses also tap contactless cards but without transfers). Nomago InterCity — intercity bus tickets, no booking fees. Arriva has no app — its site is ijpp.arriva.si. Consider the Slovenia Ticket day pass (€18, buses+trains nationwide).

Croatia + Austria

Croatia: moj ZET (Zagreb trams, QR validation, needs email registration), Traveling.com (ex-GetByBus, same platform for Plitvice/intercity), Arriva Croatia (~5% extra discount). Austria: ÖBB (buy your Zagreb→Vienna EC + airport trains here; no account needed), WienMobil (Vienna transit — registration required to buy tickets).

Hiking

Mapy.com (ex-Mapy.cz) — best free trail rendering for the Tatras and Slovenia; free tier = one offline country at a time, so stage your downloads. AllTrails — offline maps need Plus ($36/yr); fine for reviews/QA without it. Komoot — acquired in 2025, new users effectively need Premium; skip unless you already have it. Weather: Yr.no or Meteoblue for mountain forecasts.

Food, money, data

HappyCow — the vegetarian finder; free on Android since Dec 2025, one-time $4.99 on iOS, website free. Wise or Revolut for DKK/PLN/EUR (Wise's ~0.4–0.6% conversion beats Revolut's weekend markup on PLN). Data: Airalo Eurolink eSIM (one plan across all six countries) or Nomad for cheapest-per-GB.

Good to know

Practical notes

Weather & packing

Copenhagen 12–16°C + wind; Zakopane 8–16°C days / 4–7°C nights (summits 8–12°C colder); Slovenia mountains 14–17°C days, 5–9°C nights, sunset ~17:30; ~12 rainy days/month in the Oct mountains. Layers, waterproof shell, proper shoes, hat/gloves for cable cars, power bank for the long bus days.

Documents & borders

Schengen throughout, but active spot checks on the Sweden, Slovenia→Croatia and Austria borders — passport on every intercity bus/train. One Schengen visa covers all six countries; confirm its dates cover 20 Sep–7 Oct with buffer. Nothing sensitive from you went into any web search for this plan.

Money

DKK (Denmark), PLN (Poland), EUR elsewhere. Cards work nearly everywhere, but carry ~200–300 PLN cash for Zakopane minibuses, MDA drivers and milk bars, and small cash for Mostnica (€3) and Metelkova clubs.

Vegetarian phrases

"Jestem wegetarianinem/wegetarianką" (PL), "Jeg er vegetar" (DK), "Ich bin Vegetarier/in" (AT). Polish soups often hide meat broth — ask "na wywarze warzywnym?". If you're going strict no-egg (your other planner assumed that; you told me dairy & eggs are fine — pick one): "bez jajek" (PL), "brez jajc" (SI), "ohne Ei" (AT/DE), "uden æg" (DK). Egg hides in dumpling dough, breading, custard and Danish pastries. Oscypek is cheese — you're fine. Blitva (chard & potato) is the Croatian default side.

What's still weather-flexible

Day 3 (Louisiana ⇄ Malmö), Day 5 (Morskie Oko ⇄ Rusinowa), Day 7 (Nowa Huta ⇄ Wieliczka rain-swap), Day 11 (Bohinj ⇄ cave day), Day 13 (Velika Planina ⇄ Šmarna Gora). That's five built-in swap points — no day collapses in rain.

Velika Planina cable-car maintenance window (velikaplanina.si); Sljeme cable-car service start (zicarasljeme.hr); Gujžina reopened (Google Maps); Prozak 2.0 Saturday event; Belianska Cave entry slots on the day. Every other ✻ leg: operator site the night before.
